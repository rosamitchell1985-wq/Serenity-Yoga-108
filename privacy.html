Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

SerenityYoga.online ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it — in compliance with applicable U.S. law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA).

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone) when you sign up, contact us, or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Billing information processed securely by our payment provider (e.g., Stripe or PayPal). We never store full card numbers.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and class attendance, used to improve our service.
  • Zoom data when you join live classes (first name and email as required by Zoom).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide classes, memberships, and customer support.
  • To send service emails (schedules, receipts) and, with your consent, marketing emails.
  • To improve our website and keep it secure.

3. What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We never sell the personal data of seniors or any other user.

4. Sharing with service providers

We share limited data only with trusted providers needed to run our service — payment processing, email delivery, video conferencing, and website hosting — each bound by their own privacy obligations.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential site function and basic analytics. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to opt out.

6. Your rights

Depending on your state (including California and Virginia), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell it).
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

To exercise any right, email privacy@serenityyoga.online. We respond within 45 days as required by law.

7. Data security & retention

We use industry-standard encryption, secure servers, and limited staff access. We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our service or as required by law.

8. Children

Our service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided us data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@serenityyoga.online · Serenity Yoga, P.O. Box 1204, Sarasota, FL 34230.
