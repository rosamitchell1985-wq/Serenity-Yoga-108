Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how SerenityYoga.online uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember your preferences.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for sign-in sessions, security, and checkout. The site will not work properly without them.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices like staying signed in.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand (anonymously) which pages are popular so we can improve the site.

What we don't use

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not allow third-party ad networks to track you on our site.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site Settings"). Blocking essential cookies may prevent checkout or sign-in from working.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy as our site evolves; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: privacy@serenityyoga.online.
