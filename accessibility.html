Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Serenity Yoga is committed to making online yoga accessible to everyone, including older adults and people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Clear, readable fonts and high-contrast colors throughout the site
  • Full keyboard navigation and a "skip to content" link on every page
  • Alt text on all images for screen-reader users
  • Responsive design that works on tablets, phones, and computers
  • Reduced-motion support for users who prefer less animation
  • Descriptive labels on all forms and buttons
  • Video captions available on class replays

Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our site, we want to fix it. Please contact us:

  • Email: access@serenityyoga.online
  • Phone: (800) 555-0199 · Mon–Fri, 9 AM–5 PM ET

We aim to respond to accessibility requests within 3 business days.
