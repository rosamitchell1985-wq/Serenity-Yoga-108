Frequently asked questions

Do I need any special equipment?

No. A sturdy chair without wheels, comfortable clothing, and a device with internet (computer, tablet, or smartphone) are all you need. A water bottle nearby is nice to have.

I've never done yoga. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Our classes are designed for complete beginners and older adults. Every pose has seated and standing options, and you are always encouraged to rest when needed.

What if I can't attend a live class?

Every live class is recorded and added to your on-demand library within 24 hours, so you can practice whenever it suits you.

Is chair yoga safe for arthritis or joint pain?

Chair yoga is one of the gentlest forms of exercise and many members with arthritis practice comfortably. However, always consult your physician before beginning any new exercise program. See our Health Disclaimer.

How do I cancel my membership?

You can cancel anytime from your account page or by emailing support. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. See our Refund Policy.
